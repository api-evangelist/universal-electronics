# Universal Electronics

> Universal Electronics Inc. (UEI) is a global leader in universal control and sensing technologies for the smart home — the company behind the QuickSet software platform, UEI TIDE smart thermostats and sensors, the Nevo white-label control platform, and the One For All retail remote-control brand. QuickSet Cloud provides device discovery, interoperability, and entertainment control via a device knowledge graph covering roughly one million devices across 12,000 brands. Web APIs and native SDKs (Linux, Android, RDK, Android TV) are offered to OEM and service-provider partners through a gated developer portal; UEI publishes no public self-serve API.
